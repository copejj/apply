Take control of your job search pipeline. When you are sending out numerous applications across different job boards, losing track of your timeline isn't just easy, it almost seems inevitable. This site functions as an application tracking registry to catalog company profiles, contact details, and job descriptions, turning a cluttered search into an organized history.

Built out of necessity to eliminate the stress of tracking modern job hunts, the platform ensures that weeks or months down the line, you can instantly remind yourself exactly what position you applied for and when.

Core Platform Capabilities:

  • Company & Job Logging: Keep a clear record of company addresses, websites, job listing URLs, confirmation numbers, and specific position details.
  • Dynamic Status & Notes: Easily monitor where you stand by updating the active status of any application as it moves through the hiring pipeline, alongside custom notes for every role.
  • Audit-Ready Reporting: Maintain a reliable, chronological history of your weekly search activity, making tracking for compliance, personal accountability, or unemployment insurance less stressful.
